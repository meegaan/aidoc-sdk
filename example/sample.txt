DATA SECURITY POLICY – SAMPLE DOCUMENT

1. Purpose

This document describes the rules for handling confidential information within the organization.

2. Definition

“Confidential Information” means any non-public data, including customer records, financial data, and internal business plans.

3. Requirement

Employees must protect all Confidential Information from unauthorized disclosure.

4. Prohibition

Employees must not share Confidential Information with external parties without proper authorization.

5. Instruction

To access secure files, open the company portal and log in using your assigned credentials.

6. Condition

If an employee suspects that Confidential Information has been exposed, the employee must report the incident immediately.

7. Exception

Unless explicitly authorized by the compliance department, employees may not transmit Confidential Information via personal email.

8. Reference

For additional guidance, see Section 12 of the Information Security Handbook.
